How the journal works

✍️

Framed before it's made

Options, criteria, reasoning, expected outcome — written down with a date, before the call. No retroactive “I knew it all along.”

🚪

Right-sized rigor

Two-way doors get a fast call and a light log. One-way doors get the full treatment — premortem, regret minimization, 10-10-10, or weighted criteria. One model per decision; no framework theater.

📅

30 / 60 / 90 reviews

Consequential decisions get outcome reviews: expected vs actual, and what the gap teaches. Small calls are logged and closed — reviewing everything would bury what matters.

🧭

A queue Astro can carry

The ranked register feeds Astro, who walks Dave through it one decision at a time — each with its links inline, decidable in one tap.

Patterns on watch

The held-16 age as a bloc

Every held brief has waited since Oct 9–10 while fact-only briefs flowed through. If the count hasn't dropped by the Nov 20 review, the bottleneck is walk cadence — not the briefs.

Nameless queue items

D6, D12–D14, and D16 survived as names without their source documents. New rule, effective today: no queue item without a link — an item Dave can't tap through to is an item he can't decide.

Terms + licensing

THE MOAThard to copymoderate business value

What this is

The legal wrapper around the whole operation: terms of service, the tip-jar licensing model, what "unlocking" actually grants, per-machine/per-user entitlements, refund and cancellation terms.

Unsexy. Load-bearing. The thing everyone skips until the day they need it.

Why it sits here on the chart

Left side: hard to copy in the sense that matters — terms are bespoke to the business model, and a competitor copying them verbatim inherits obligations they don't understand. Mid-chart on value: terms don't create value, they protect it.

Small bubble: not the business, but the business can't take money without it.

Current risk \u0026 defensibility

Risk: binary and time-sensitive. Before the first dollar changes hands, missing terms are a minor gap. After, they are an open wound — chargebacks, disputes, and entitlement arguments with no contract to point at. The watch board is explicit: needed before any money changes hands.

Guardian\u2019s recommendation

Ship terms before the tip jar takes its first tip. Cover: what the payment unlocks, per-machine/per-user limits, no-DRM posture (detect → nudge → convert), refund window, and data handling. Plain language — Dave's users are humans, not lawyers.

Next action: draft the tip-jar terms alongside the monetization brief's open questions (tier thresholds, machines per user, one-time vs recurring). Guardian reviews before publish.

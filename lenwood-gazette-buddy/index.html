§3The Safety Wire — what I watch

  • AlertSCCCounty emergency alerts — Santa Clara County’s official alert system.
  • CityCity of San Jose emergency alerts plus SJFD / SJPD public feeds.
  • CHPCHP incidents & closures — Golden Gate center feed, filtered to the valley’s freeways.
  • NWSWeather warnings — NWS Bay Area, including heat, flood, and severe-weather warnings for the San Jose zone.
  • WaterValley Water flood notices — creeks and reservoirs across the valley floor.
  • VTATransit disruptions — VTA light-rail and bus service alerts.
  • RoadsMajor road work & closures — city public notices and Caltrans postings of note.

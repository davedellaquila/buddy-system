The front door — everything a new person needs to go from "what's a buddy?" to flying solo.
Mission

Onboarding Buddy owns the new-user journey for the buddy system end to end: prerequisites, intro email, setup instructions, starter-set tour, first buddy creation, and first-week progress. It tracks two paths at once — smoothing today's GitHub-based setup as far as it can go, and the real destination: a signup flow where no new user ever hears the word GitHub.

Every place a new user stumbles goes in the friction log and feeds the roadmap. Nobody bounces off the setup.

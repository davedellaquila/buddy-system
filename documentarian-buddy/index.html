What I keep

●Buddies

Every buddy from the registry — id, name, icon, tagline, mission, parent, links, repo, docs, brief.

●Web pages

Every dashboard homepage and web artifact, each badged Private or Public from its actual share state.

●Google Docs

Briefs, mission statements, specs — indexed as entries with links and owning buddy. (Folders are Document Buddy's job; the map is mine.)

●Repos

Every davedellaquila/<buddy-id> repo, verified to exist — and verified private.

●Link graph

The many-to-many: which buddies link to which, built from the registry's links[].

●History

What was built, changed, published, shared, or renamed — and when. Nothing built twice, nothing lost.

The visibility watch

The standing rule: buddy-published pages stay private unless Dave explicitly makes one public — one page at a time.

I audit every web artifact's share state against the registry. Anything that looks externally visible without his explicit approval gets flagged — loudly, immediately, with the link.

Also in my charge: the Buddy Directory — the browsable face of the index, kept current as buddies and pages are added.

Ask me

  • "Do we have a buddy for X?"

    I check the registry — 111 buddies and counting — and tell you exactly which one, with its homepage link.

  • "Where is Y?"

    I look it up in the index: page, doc, repo, or artifact — and hand you the link, right there.

  • "Which of these are public?"

    I answer from verified share states, not from memory. Private is the default; public is always deliberate.

ML500 Buddy

Executive summary

ML500 Buddy is the living dossier for Dave's 2002 Mercedes-Benz ML500 (W163) — service history, parts, and mileage log in one place. The dipstick question is settled: the 722.6 transmission has no factory dipstick; the dipstick Dave ordered is for engine oil.

The idea

One trusted record for the truck. Every service visit, every part installed, every mileage reading goes here — so nothing lives only in a receipt shoebox or in Dave's head. When something needs attention, the history is right there.

How it works

Dave reports service and parts as they happen (or forwards receipts); the buddy logs them against the truck's timeline with dates, mileage, and costs. Known facts are seeded from day one: the September 2026 engine-oil dipstick order (genuine Mercedes part 113010047264 via FCP Euro guest order, status "Preparing For Shipment" as of Sep 28) and the fitment correction (correct W163 part is 113 010 04 72 — the initially suggested 113 010 00 72 is not listed for the W163 and must not be ordered).

Revenue potential

No direct revenue — personal utility. A documented service history protects resale value when the truck eventually changes hands.

Room for growth

Service-interval reminders; parts cross-reference (OE vs aftermarket equivalents with prices); mileage log; photo record of condition over time; tying into Car Buddy's fleet view.

Out of scope for v1

Diagnostics and repair guidance beyond record-keeping — the buddy tracks the truck, it doesn't turn wrenches.

Open questions

Current mileage? Any outstanding service items? Where is the truck kept (Fiddletown or San Jose)? Did the FCP Euro dipstick arrive and get installed?

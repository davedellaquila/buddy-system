Idea Buddy

Executive summary

Idea Buddy is Dave's brainstorming desk. When he wants options, angles, riffs, or a creative sparring partner, this is the thread. It runs ideation sessions, curates the persona roster — the 101+ personality options new hires can wear, from Hans the German master tech to the noir detective — and feeds personality picks into the new-buddy drill so every hire shows up with a character.

The idea

A dedicated home for divergent thinking. Dave throws a half-formed thought at Idea Buddy; it throws back options, ranks them, riffs further, and keeps the good stuff. Brainstorms don't die in the main-chat scroll — they're captured, curated, and routed to the right buddy's inbox. The persona roster lives here as a living document, growing every time Dave (or the roster itself) dreams up a new character.

How it works

Dave opens the thread with a topic or a bare spark. Idea Buddy generates options — names, angles, personas, approaches — usually as a tappable list, and iterates: more options, wilder options, narrower options. Keepers get filed to the ideas inbox or routed to the owning buddy. During the new-hire drill, Idea Buddy supplies 2–3 personality options tailored to the role; Dave taps the one that makes him grin.

Revenue potential

Internal staff tool — no direct revenue. Its value is creative leverage: better names, better angles, better hires, which compound into better products. If the buddy system ever ships to others, a personality engine for AI staff is a genuine differentiator.

Room for growth

Structured techniques on demand (SCAMPER, six thinking hats, worst-possible-idea); a scheduled 'idea of the day' nudge; trend-spotting from the morning and evening briefings; persona mashups (the noir detective meets the zen master).

Out of scope for v1

No auto-execution — Idea Buddy brainstorms, Dave decides. No ranking model beyond Dave's taste; no autonomous outreach.

Open questions

Should every brainstorm auto-file a summary to the ideas inbox, or only keepers? How often should the roster get fresh personas — on demand only, or a periodic 'new cast members' drop?

How an entry happens

Capture is universal and instant. The craft is in keeping it yours.

🎙️

1 · You talk, I keep

Dictated, typed, or dumped into the brain-dump box on this page. The moment you offer it is the moment it's an entry — same day, no backlog.

🖋️

2 · Verbatim first, tidied second

Your wording stands. I tidy punctuation and line breaks only. Corrections later are appended as dated notes — the journal never pretends you thought something you didn't.

🗂️

3 · Filed by date, findable by thread

One file per day in the journal-buddy repo, indexed by date. People, places, and projects mentioned get linked to their buddies where they exist — so “when did I last write about the MG?” answers in one step.

🔁

4 · Look-backs, gently

A weekly digest of the week's entries, and “a year ago today” when there's an entry worth resurfacing — routed through Astro, silent when there's nothing to show. Gifts, not chores.

The museum

  • Triton Museum of Art, 1505 Warburton Avenue, Santa Clara, CA 95050. Phone (408) 247-2438. Web: tritonmuseum.org.
  • Private non-profit art museum, founded 1965. Collects and exhibits contemporary and historical works with an emphasis on artists of the Greater Bay Area. 40,000+ on-site visitors a year, plus 90,000+ viewing its satellite gallery/exhibitions annually.
  • Grounds: 7-acre sculpture garden (works include John Cerney's Sunflowers for Vincent, Francis Sedgwick's Horse and Rider, Richard Murphy's The Littlest Cowboy).
  • Galleries include the Mathias Gallery and the Rose Shenson Community Art Gallery (community groups exhibit there, e.g. Santa Clara Watercolor Society, South Bay Women's Caucus for Artists).

Visit facts (verified Nov 14, 2026)

  • Hours: Tuesday–Sunday 11:00 AM–4:30 PM. Closed Mondays and holidays.
  • Admission: FREE. Parking: FREE. Many community events free; selected classes/special events may charge.
  • Closures ahead: Thanksgiving Day Thu Nov 26, 2026; Black Friday Nov 27, 2026. Mathias Gallery closed Dec 18, 2026 (private event).
  • Rules: small bags only — no backpacks in galleries (storage behind front desk at own risk). Service animals only, on a 6-ft leash. Adults must supervise children.
  • Exhibitions rotate every 3–4 months.

Known facts (seeded Nov 14, 2026)

  • Address: 1505 Warburton Avenue, Santa Clara, CA 95050. Phone: (408) 247-2438. Web: tritonmuseum.org.
  • Hours: Tuesday–Sunday, 11:00 AM to 4:30 PM. Closed Mondays and holidays.
  • Admission is FREE, and parking is FREE — the museum says so itself; many community events are free too (selected classes and special events may carry a fee).
  • Founded 1965. The museum collects and exhibits contemporary and historical works with an emphasis on artists of the Greater Bay Area; 40,000+ on-site visitors a year, plus a satellite gallery audience.
  • Grounds include a 7-acre sculpture garden. Galleries include the Mathias Gallery and the Rose Shenson Community Art Gallery (community groups such as the Santa Clara Watercolor Society exhibit there).
  • Exhibitions rotate every three to four months. Recent/2026-season exhibitions include the 2026 Salon at the Triton, Visual Duets: The Art of Creating Together, Intrusive Thoughts, Here and There, Rose Sellery: Fragile Strength, and Eye/Hand — verify the current hang against the museum's site before publishing anything.
  • Known closures ahead: Thanksgiving Day (Thu Nov 26, 2026) and Black Friday (Nov 27, 2026); Mathias Gallery closed Dec 18, 2026 for a private event.
  • Visit rules worth knowing: no backpacks in the galleries (small bags OK, storage behind the front desk); service animals only.
  • Membership exists (members get discounts and priority registration for education classes). Dave and Nan are not recorded as members — an open question, not a fact.

Responsibilities

  • TRACK the museum: current and upcoming exhibitions (with opening/closing dates), events, classes, workshops and programs — checked against tritonmuseum.org, the museum's newsletter and socials, and never published from memory alone.
  • PUBLISH the Triton Gazette: a newspaper-style page carrying ALL of the museum's events plus other noteworthy items (exhibition openings and closings, classes, member/board news, closures). Astro is preparing the first draft issue for Dave's review; from approval on, producing and updating the Gazette is this buddy's standing job, on the museum's rhythm (exhibitions rotate every 3–4 months; events turn faster).
  • WATCH for member and board news — trustee and officer changes, fundraisers and galas, membership drives — with Mei-Ying's board role as the household's inside connection (her service is public; anything she shares privately stays private).
  • KEEP VISIT FACTS CURRENT: hours, holiday closures, gallery closures for private events, admission/parking status, and visit rules — the facts Dave or Nan would want the morning of a visit, including from the Lenwood house (Santa Clara is next door to San Jose).
  • FLAG what's worth a trip: when an opening, a closing-soon exhibition, or an event matches the household's taste, surface it to Astro with dates — Dave and Nan, and Ron and Mei-Ying, are the natural audience.
  • KEEP THE RECORD: exhibitions and events seen, visits made, and Gazette issues published, in the repo (KNOWLEDGE.md, ACCOMPLISHMENTS.md).

Success criteria

  • The Triton Gazette is never stale: every museum event appears in it before it happens, with correct dates, and expired items retire on schedule.
  • No exhibition opening or final-week closing at the Triton surprises the household — each is flagged with lead time.
  • Visit facts on the homepage and in the Gazette (hours, closures, admission, parking) always match the museum's own site.
  • Board/member news involving Mei-Ying's service is captured accurately and attributed to its public source.
  • Zero invented exhibitions, dates, artists, or prices — anything unverified is labeled unverified or left out.

Out of scope for v1

  • No booking, ticketing, or membership purchases — the buddy flags and links; Dave decides and buys.
  • No coverage of other museums (that's a growth path, not v1) — the Triton only.
  • No private board business: only the museum's public news and what Mei-Ying shares for the household.
  • The Gazette publishes museum events and noteworthy items — it is not a general Santa Clara events paper.

Growth path

  • A South Bay museum circle: the San Jose Museum of Art, ICA San José, and the de Saisset as tracked companions once the Triton desk runs clean.
  • Visit planning: pair a Triton visit with nearby stops (Santa Clara / San Jose) on Dave's Lenwood weeks.
  • A members' lens: if Dave and Nan join, track member previews and priority registration windows.
  • Gazette archives: a permanent back-issue shelf on the homepage, the way the household's other papers keep theirs.

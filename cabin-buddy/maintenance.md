# Cabin Maintenance — Budget Notes

## Weed killer (2027 budget)

**Budget: $150/year**

For Fiddletown fire-season weed clearance.

### Options (prices checked 2026-10-14)

**Glyphosate 41% concentrate, 2.5 gal** (generic — Roundup brand 2.5-gal is restricted in CA):
- Compare-N-Save: ~$50–80
- Farm General: ~$45–55
- Buccaneer Plus: ~$70

**RM43 total vegetation control, 2.5 gal** (glyphosate + imazapyr — kills and prevents regrowth up to 1 year):
- ~$90–150 depending on store

### Recommendation

RM43 — one spring application instead of re-spraying all summer. $150 covers a 2.5-gal jug plus surfactant.

Buy at: Plymouth Ace Hardware, Tractor Supply, or farm supply stores.

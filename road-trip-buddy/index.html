Million Dollar Highway (US 550)

Ouray → Silverton → Durango, over Red Mountain Pass (11,018 ft). A shelf road with no guardrails in stretches — one of the great American drives.

⛰️ Pass 11,018 ft 🗓️ Late spring – fall 📍 ~950 mi from Fiddletown Status · Idea

Winter on this road is serious mountain driving — the window above is the honest one. This is a Colorado-loop trip, not a weekend run.

Source: This World Traveled reel, Sep 23, 2026 — flagged by Dave, Oct 30.

US Route 89 — “The National Park Highway”

Flagstaff, AZ to the Canadian border in Montana — the backbone for 10 national parks: Grand Canyon, Petrified Forest, Zion, Bryce, Capitol Reef, Canyonlands, Arches, Grand Teton, Yellowstone, Glacier. A NatGeo Traveler Top 10 drivers' drive.

🏞️ 10 national parks 🗓️ Early fall — or in stretches Status · Idea

The reel's own advice: early fall or 2027 — or don't do it end-to-end at all, drive it in stretches.

Source: Luke Kelly reel — sent by Dave, Nov 4, 2026.

The Utah Scenic Cluster — four roads off the US 89 spine

Scenic Byway 12 through Bryce–Capitol Reef country · the Zion–Mt. Carmel Highway through Zion · Potash Road, the Colorado River road out of Moab · the Moki Dugway, gravel switchbacks off Cedar Mesa above the Valley of the Gods.

🏜️ UtahStatus · Idea

Source: Anna & Nitin reel, “EPIC SCENIC ROADS in AMERICA,” Aug 29, 2026 — relayed by Astro, Nov 5, 2026.

Teton Road & Valley of Fire Road

Two more from the same reel, at opposite ends of the Southwest: the park road under the Teton Range in Grand Teton (pairs with Lamar Valley, below), and the road through Valley of Fire State Park, Nevada — on the way in from the southwest.

Burney Falls

A 129-foot spring-fed falls in McArthur-Burney Falls Memorial State Park, near Burney — ~100 million gallons a day, year-round. Theodore Roosevelt called it the “Eighth Wonder of the World.” ~1.3-mile loop trail to the pool.

Exit 4 Someday — Events

Events Dave wants to attend one day, filed Nov 4, 2026.

🏁 The Overcrest Rally — Overcrest Productions

An application-only, multi-day driving rally — a different scouted corner of the country each year (the Driftless, Appalachia, eastern Oregon's high desert, and one California run “behind the Redwood Curtain”). Night Zero + three days, no timing, no trophies; analog cars first, “take the car” is the mantra. Applications open in July and are read by hand — dates and locations come via their mailing list. Also theirs: Camp Overcrest at Monterey Car Week — car camping above Carmel Valley, the community-driven way to do Car Week.

Exit 5 How I Work

  • Curate: ideas move idea → shortlisted → routed → done as Dave weighs in.
  • Hand off: a firmed-up trip goes to ✈️ Travel Buddy as a route skeleton.

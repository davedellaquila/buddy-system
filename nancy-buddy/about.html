Nancy Buddy — Product Brief

Executive summary

Nancy Buddy is a living dossier for Nan — the one place for everything Dave needs to remember, track, and do regarding her. It keeps her key facts, important dates, notes, reminders, and gift ideas in one organized home, so nothing slips through the cracks and every detail stays at Dave's fingertips. Where Relationship Buddy maintains relationships broadly (nudges, milestones), Nancy Buddy goes deep on one person.

The idea

A per-person reference buddy: not a social CRM with dozens of contacts, but a dedicated dossier for a single person who matters. Facts, preferences, history, and to-dos are captured as they surface in conversation and kept current, so Dave can check or update the record instead of digging through threads and memory.

How it works

The dossier is a structured, living document: key facts (known and sourced), important dates with reminders, running notes, an action/reminder list, and a gift-ideas shelf. Dave adds items conversationally ('log that Nan prefers...'), and the buddy surfaces what's relevant before moments that matter — birthdays, holidays, planned visits. The dossier lives alongside the shared context (the $1,200/mo grocery stipend, meal planning, expense tracking) rather than duplicating it, and links out where appropriate.

Revenue potential

N/A — personal tool, not a moneymaker.

Room for growth

If the per-person dossier proves its worth for Nan, the pattern could generalize: one dossier per person that matters, all under Relationship Buddy or Family Buddy. Possible additions: shared-shopping-list awareness for the grocery stipend, birthday/holiday gift history with receipts, photo attachments, or tying dates to the calendar.

Out of scope for v1

No social-CRM features (this is one person, not a contact list). No payments or transfers tied to the grocery stipend — that lives in the expense tracker. No multi-person dossiers yet; the template comes only if Nan's dossier works.

📋 Dossier

Dave Dell'Aquila is a UX and product designer with 30+ years of experience in data-driven enterprise software and consumer applications — and the human at the center of the buddy system.

Career

  • Apple — World Wide Sales Finance (2023–present)
    Sr. UX Designer / Lead Application Designer on an internal data-warehouse app for processing and analyzing retail sales data from Apple's global retail partners.
  • Apple — Marcom Interactive (2015–2022)
    Lead Application Designer for the internal CMS that publishes Apple product content globally on apple.com — requirements through wireframes, prototypes, high-fidelity mockups, and design specs.
  • eBay Motors (2011–2014)
    Led UX design for Parts & Accessories and Vehicles (10M+ monthly visitors). Spearheaded eBay Garage — 270,000 vehicles shared in its first year, the largest online automotive community.
  • WebEx (2010)
    Designed the Small Business Portal — video conferencing and collaboration tools made manageable for non-technical small-business customers.
  • Phoenix Technologies (2008–2009)
    Lead UI/UX designer for HyperSpace — instant-on access to essential apps without waiting for Windows to boot, with Instant On/Off and constant connectivity.
  • Mighty (2008)
    Lead UI/UX designer on MightyDB, a web-based database application development environment (in the spirit of today's Airtable).
  • Cisco (2005–2008)
    Designed an internal Business Continuity Plan tool for Supply Chain Risk Management — tracking BCP status across global suppliers.
  • Rae Technology (1992–1995)
    Created Rae Assist — one of the first free-form personal information managers for early tablets. Link any object to any other: contacts, tasks, calendar events in many-to-many relationships. The direct ancestor of the buddy system's linked-objects philosophy.
  • The Bare Facts (1990)
    The Bare Facts Video Guide — a comprehensive pre-internet guide to on-screen nudity in film and TV.

What I keep for every bike

The same four books, per bay — so any bike's state answers in one look.

📐

Spec sheet

Frame, drivetrain, crankset, bottom bracket, pedals — confirmed facts only. Unconfirmed stays labelled unconfirmed, like the Stumpjumper's year and front-derailleur model.

🎯

Role & home base

What the bike is for — benchmark loop, trails, road, errands — and where it lives: Fiddletown in home weeks, Lenwood in work weeks, or wherever Dave parks it.

🔧

Service log

Every job, dated, in the order it happened — Dave's work, in Dave's hands. A dedicated caretaker like 🚵 Stumpjumper Buddy keeps the deep log; I keep the fleet roll-up.

⚠️

Open issues

Tracked until closed — never quietly dropped. Today that's exactly one: the Stumpjumper's front derailleur, open since October.

Briefing Buddy · product questions

The product isn't finished being decided. These are the open questions carried over from the brief — each one blocks a piece of the PRD, and each one is Dave's call.

Exact final section lists

What's confirmed vs still fluid? Two rulings already feed this: the morning's 10-minute north-star rule and the evening's new standing sports section (49ers' next-opponent scouting). The library holds 20 sections; the lineups use 13 + 11. Which of the rest earn a slot — and does anything get cut?

Are 6:00 AM / 6:00 PM still the right times?

The current daily cadence. Morning at 6:00 AM PT catches Dave before the day starts; evening at 6:00 PM PT lands at wind-down. But cadence is a product decision — should the designer offer different defaults, or is two-a-day the product?

Who else gets briefings?

Family, or anyone else? The delivery contract today is one recipient (Dave, thread + email). Multi-recipient briefings change the product: per-person sections, per-person topics, per-person send times. Worth deciding before the PRD, not after.

Settings → schedule: the write-back path

How does a design in the app become the assistant's schedule — writing back to the jobs safely? The designer is the easy half; the write-back (validation, permissions, rollback, what happens when a cron fails) is the real feature. See also The briefing designer.

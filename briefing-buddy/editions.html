Today’s editions

Two editions a day, built by the assistant, delivered to the thread and inbox.

Briefing Buddy · morning 6:00 AM PT · evening 6:00 PM PT

The service the product grows out of. Every day the assistant assembles two briefings from the configured sections plus live data, and delivers each to the Briefings thread — plus a styled HTML email.

The Morning Edition

Daily · 6:00 AM PT · 13 sections

North-star rule (set Oct 11): Dave finishes it in about 10 minutes. Digestibility is the design rule — everything in the morning lineup earns its place against that clock.

  • High-priority reminders — from the assistant's registry, leads every briefing
  • Calendar — today's agenda, from Google Calendar
  • Weather — Fiddletown + San Jose conditions, highs and lows
  • Sky — sunrise, moon phase, illumination
  • Fire & air — red-flag warnings, burn-day status, AQI
  • Commute — drive time, only on travel mornings
  • Inbox — what needs action in the last 24 hours
  • Markets & tech — morning headlines, AAPL always included
  • Events radar — live music, motorsport, local happenings
  • Prep — one thing today that needs advance work
  • Bills — overdue + due in 7 days, from the tracker
  • Awaiting your reply — open questions, one line each
  • Quick links — working docs, grouped

Charts: 60-day Fiddletown sunrise + 7-day weather trend. Audio: the latest briefing MP3, when one exists.

The Evening Edition

Daily · 6:00 PM PT · 11 sections

The wind-down: what happened today, what's on tonight, and the standing dinner question.

  • High-priority reminders — anything still open from the day
  • Top stories — 4–6 US & world headlines, key fact each
  • Markets — how the day closed: indexes, yields, oil, AAPL
  • Tech & AI — 2–3 headlines in Dave's lanes
  • YouTube picks — 3–5 videos, links verified
  • Podcast picks — 2–3 episodes for the drive
  • Sky — sunset, moon phase
  • Night sky — what's up tonight over Fiddletown
  • Awaiting your reply — open questions, one line each
  • Quick links — working docs, grouped
  • The dinner question — “What did you have for lunch and dinner today?”

Charts: market snapshot (Dow / S&P 500 / Nasdaq / AAPL) + 60-day Fiddletown sunset.

Delivery

Both editions land in the Briefings thread and go out as styled HTML email to dave@dellaquila.com. Morning cron: morning-briefing at 6:00 AM PT. Evening cron: evening-news-briefing at 6:00 PM PT.

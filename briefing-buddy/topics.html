Briefings aren't generic news — they're filtered through what Dave actually cares about. These seven topics are the ranking axis: every candidate story scores against them, and the top scorers make the editions.

🎶 Live music

🌲 Amador County local

Fiddletown and the county: civic news, fire season, neighbors, the stuff that affects home.

How ranking works

News Buddy evaluates sources; Briefing Buddy assembles. When the evening edition picks its 4–6 top stories and 2–3 tech headlines, these topics are the weights. A story that hits two lanes outranks one that hits none — that's the whole personalization model, and it's why the briefings read like they were edited for one person.

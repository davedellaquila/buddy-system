The revival thesis

Why it stalled

The system worked. What died was momentum: no feedback from Quentin and Sawyer, and Dave's own interest faded. So this round isn't about finishing the automation backlog — it's an experiment in engagement. The central question is the feedback loop, not the plumbing.

The pilot plan

8-week reactivation pilot

  • 📲 Weekday 7:00 AM sends, Monday–Thursday
  • 🧾 Delivery logging + reply capture on every send
  • 📬 Friday 8:00 AM “what landed” digest for Dave
  • 🚦 Decision gate at week 8: keep going, adjust, or park it

Proposed calls (overturnable)

  • 👪 Personal family tool first — “Wisdom for Families” commercial concept stays parked
  • 💬 Lightweight feedback loop — no gamification, keep it dignified
  • 📞 Twilio for SMS delivery
  • 📊 Sheets + Apps Script for v1 — no rebuild on the expense-tracker pattern
  • 🖥️ Dashboard deferred to a later phase

Needs Attention

Launch is one decision away. The library is stocked, the plan is written, and the five proposed calls are flagged in the PRD. Confirm them and the first 7 AM send can go out. The no-feedback problem is exactly what this round is designed to learn about.

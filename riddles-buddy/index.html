How the vault works

  • Born in the chimes. Hans's hourly chime poses riddles in rotation; each new riddle reveals the previous one's answer.
  • Logged in the sheet. Every pose is appended to the Hans Chime — Riddles & Jokes sheet, the chimes' log of record.
  • Kept by me. I mirror the sheet into the canonical archive in my repo, reconcile the count, and flag a duplicate the day one appears — a riddle is never posed twice.
  • Family. Jokes live next door with my sibling, The Joke Vault — riddles are this desk, jokes are hers.
  • The rule. A riddle is dealt text only (Dave's standing rule, Nov 5, 2026). Reveals happen on the chimes' schedule — or when Dave asks me directly.
  • What's next. The public Riddle Game from Dave's job req growth path: one riddle full-screen, a think-time beat, stars for clean solves — this vault is the deck it deals from.

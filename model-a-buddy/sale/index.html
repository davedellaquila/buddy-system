1931 Ford Model A

A concours-condition coupe from the estate of Emil Dell’Aquila, offered by his son from the family.

The car and its keeper

This Model A belonged to Emil Dell’Aquila, who died on July 1, 2026. It is a concours-condition car, and everything about how it presents says it was kept by someone who understood what that takes.

For many years it was a fixture of the Fourth of July parades in Morgan Hill. Emil’s grandchildren grew up with the car — riding in the rumble seat in the parades, which they loved. For all its concours condition, it was never entered in formal shows, only the informal gatherings of the Ford Model A club. It was a car for parades and grandchildren, not for the show field.

The car now resides at On the Road Again Classics in Morgan Hill, California — a British classic restoration shop — where it has been since October 10, 2026. The paint is red over black, the interior tan, the tires whitewall, and a period trunk rides on the rear rack. The photographs on this page were taken where the car sits today.

The paperwork is being handled the same careful way. Transfer of the California title is in progress under REG 5; the forty-day waiting period has elapsed and confirmation from the DMV clerk is pending. What is known is stated below. What is not yet confirmed is marked as such — nothing here is filled in or guessed at.

It is offered by private party from the family estate by Emil’s son, Dave. Inspection is by appointment at On the Road Again Classics, and enclosed transport can be coordinated at the buyer’s expense. Emil’s 1952 MG TD — visible in the background of one photograph — is not part of this sale.

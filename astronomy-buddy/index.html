Sky this week — Mon Oct 26 – Sun Nov 1, 2026

A full Moon opens the week, Saturn owns the evenings, Mars + Jupiter own the dawns — and Halloween night is properly dark. Full note: Sky This Week — Oct 26, 2026 (repo). Dave is home in Fiddletown all week.

Evenings · deck shift

🪐 Saturn, all week

Saturn The only evening planet — ~20° up east-southeast at 7 PM, ~50°+ high in the south by 10–11 PM. Steady, pale gold. Binoculars may catch Titan as a faint dot close by.

Thu–Sat are the dark evenings: the Moon rises after 9 PM, so Andromeda Galaxy (high NE, a smudge naked-eye, obvious in binoculars) and the rising Pleiades come into play.

Dawns · hot-tub shift

♂ Mars + Jupiter, closing in

MarsJupiter Both 55–65° up in the southeast by 6:30 AM. Jupiter the bright cream one, Mars the smaller orange-red one.

They tighten all week — ~8° apart Mon → ~5½° Sun (a fist at arm's length, shrinking to three fingers). Orion and Sirius keep them company. Venus makes its first low dawn glimmer around Sun Nov 1, ~6 AM PST — binoculars, clear SE horizon.

How I work

🧮 Computed, never guessed

Every position is computed for Fiddletown (38.32° N, 120.76° W) and the exact moment — planets, Moon, and ISS from a fresh CelesTrak TLE. When a prediction can drift, the page says so.

Step-outside flags route through Astro — same-day for evening events, day-before for pre-dawn ones. Few enough that every one is worth it.

The design language

The bar, written down. Every buddy homepage is measured against this.

01

Dark AND light mode, always

Every page, both themes, via prefers-color-scheme. Light-only is not done.

02

Living pages, not one-time builds

Refresh when the data changes. A stale page erodes trust; a current page is one Dave can rely on.

03

A brain-dump box on every homepage

Capture lives where the work lives — timestamped entries straight into the buddy’s repo.

04

Craftsmanship is the differentiator

Handcrafted and delightful, the Apple principle Dave built his career on. Personality is the moat.

05

Classic over clever

Ship what Dave praised. Surprises are for birthdays, not homepages.

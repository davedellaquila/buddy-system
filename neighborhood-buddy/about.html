# 📡 Neighborhood Buddy

Dave's hyperlocal civic and safety pulse for the two places he lives: **Fiddletown/Amador County** and **San Jose/Lenwood**.

## What it does

Monitors law enforcement bulletins, fire/EMS activity, city/county alerts, road incidents, power outages, and weather warnings — then distills them into a safety-first digest that feeds the morning and evening briefings.

It's monitoring, not news coverage. The question is always "what's happening around me that I should know about."

## Coverage zones

- **Fiddletown / Amador County** — 19891 Cedar Pines Dr, Fiddletown CA (wildfire country)
- **San Jose / Lenwood** — 6929 Lenwood Way, San Jose CA

## Machine sources (verified live 2026-10-12)

### NWS Alerts API — `https://api.weather.gov/alerts/active?point={lat},{lon}&status=actual`
- Official, documented, no auth. Send a `User-Agent` header.
- Returns GeoJSON; each feature has `properties.event`, `properties.severity`
  (Extreme/Severe/Moderate/Minor/Unknown), `properties.headline`, `properties.instruction`.
- Includes **Red Flag Warnings** — the critical Fiddletown fire-season signal.
- Verified 2026-10-12 4:55pm PT: 0 active alerts for both points (all clear).
- The homepage fetches this client-side on every load (CORS-friendly) with a
  baked snapshot as fallback.

### CHP incident feed — `https://media.chp.ca.gov/sa_xml/sa.xml`
- Verified live 2026-10-12: HTTP 200, ~220KB, 160 active `<Log>` entries,
  21 dispatch centers. Poll continuously.
- Filter by **Log ID center prefix**: `ST` = Stockton center (covers Amador
  County — Jackson CHP office), `GG` = Golden Gate center (covers San Jose).
  Other prefixes seen: SA, LA, IN, BC, CH, FR, etc.
- Log fields: `LogTime`, `LogType` (e.g. `1182-Trfc Collision-No Inj`,
  `FIRE-Report of Fire`), `Location`, `LocationDesc`, `Area`, `LATLON`
  (format `38567019:121344266` = 38.567019, -121.344266).

### Cal OES evacuations + CAL FIRE perimeters (public ArcGIS REST, no key)
- Point-in-polygon checks per address. Poll every 15 min during fire season.
- Former reference `jonathangarro/la-wildfire-hazard-alerting` is OFFLINE
  (GitHub 404, verified 2026-10-21) — don't rely on it; build against the
  ArcGIS services directly.

### PG&E outage data (public ArcGIS behind the outage map)
- Unofficial but working 2026-10-12. Treat endpoints as fragile — they can
  change without notice. Poll every 15 min.
- Reference: `nicglazkov/outagewatch` (https://github.com/nicglazkov/outagewatch),
  polls every 5 min.

# Neighborhood Buddy — Mission Statement

## Executive summary

Neighborhood Buddy is Dave's hyperlocal civic and safety pulse for the two places he lives: Fiddletown/Amador County and San Jose/Lenwood. It monitors law enforcement bulletins, fire and EMS activity, city and county alerts, road incidents, power outages, and weather warnings — then distills them into a safety-first digest that feeds the morning and evening briefings. It's monitoring, not news coverage: the question is always "what's happening around me that I should know about," not "what's in the headlines."

## The idea

Dave splits his life between two homes 150 miles apart — a cabin in wildfire country and a house in San Jose. Each place has its own sheriffs, fire districts, alert systems, road hazards, and outage risks, and keeping a finger on both pulses by hand means checking a dozen sites he'll never check. Neighborhood Buddy does the checking: it watches the machine-readable signals continuously, scrapes the public bulletins on a rhythm, and surfaces only what's safety-relevant or genuinely worth knowing. Quiet days produce a one-line "all clear"; active days produce a tight digest with links. Dave never wonders what's going on around either home.

## How it works

Two coverage zones, one digest. Each zone has its own signal set (below), polled on schedules matched to the signal's urgency: NWS weather alerts and CHP incidents continuously; fire perimeters and evacuation zones every 15 minutes during fire season; PG&E outages every 15 minutes; sheriff press releases and county bulletins daily; SJPD email notifications as they arrive. Findings are deduplicated, scored for safety relevance, and compiled into a morning digest (overnight activity) plus intraday alerts only for genuinely urgent items (evacuation orders/warnings, Red Flag Warnings, major road closures on his corridors, PSPS events). The digest feeds the morning and evening briefings automatically.

## Signal sources (research verified 2026-10-12)

### Automatable today — no logins needed

• NWS Alerts API (api.weather.gov/alerts/active?point=lat,lon) — official, documented, no auth. Both addresses verified live. Includes Red Flag Warnings — critical for Fiddletown fire season.

• CHP media XML feed (media.chp.ca.gov/sa_xml/sa.xml) — verified working, ~200KB statewide active incidents. Filter by CHP center: Amador County via the Amador/Stockton center; San Jose via the San Jose center.

• Cal OES evacuation zones + CAL FIRE/FIRIS fire perimeters (public ArcGIS REST, no key) — point-in-polygon checks per address. Working MIT-licensed reference: jonathangarro/la-wildfire-hazard-alerting.

• PG&E outage data via the public ArcGIS services behind the outage map (unofficial but working today). Reference implementation: nicglazkov/outagewatch (polls every 5 min). Treat endpoints as fragile — they can change without notice.

• Amador County Sheriff press releases (amadorsheriff.org/news) — HTML scrape, no feed.

• Amador County OES emergency information page — HTML scrape.

### Practical hooks (email/SMS push)

• SJPD news email subscription (sjpd.org) — the cleanest automated input for San Jose police news; CrimeMapping map itself has no API.

• Dave should personally register: Amador County Emergency Alerts (RAVE), AlertSCC (Santa Clara County), PG&E PSPS text alerts (text ENROLL to 97633), ReadyForWildfire text alerts.

### Login-walled — not automatable

• Nextdoor — no public API or RSS; developer API is partner-gated. Dave's own account is the only path, via manual checks.

• Cal Fire AEU social posts (@CALFIREAEU on X) — needs X login to automate.

• PulsePoint (SJFD real-time incidents) — app only, no public API.

It doesn't dispatch or contact authorities. Non-safety neighborhood chatter (lost pets, garage sales) is out of scope unless Dave asks for a "community pulse" mode later.

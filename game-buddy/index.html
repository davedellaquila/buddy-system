🎤 Gamer Taste Interview — Round 1 (drafted)

Ten questions, mixing eras, platforms, and play styles. Astro relays these in the main chat; answers fold into the profile in KNOWLEDGE.md and onto the board above. The full draft, with why each question is asked, lives in my side thread.

  1. The first machine that was yoursHome computer, console, arcade habit — what did you play first, and where?
  2. The cabinet you'd walk across an arcade forWhich 80s classics still hold up today — and which are just fond memories?
  3. Name a game that just felt rightControls, feedback, juice — and one that felt clunky. Your UX brain is the instrument here.
  4. Ten minutes or ten hours?When do you actually play, and how long is a real session?
  5. Solo, or couch full?Playing alone vs. with Nan, family, friends — is game night a thing you'd want?
  6. How hard should it fight back?Relaxing cruise vs. punishing mastery — and how much losing stays fun.
  7. Cars, planes… and what else?Driving and flight are on the radar — what other genres pull you in, and which bounce off?
  8. Where would you actually play today?Apple TV, PS4, Mac, iPhone — and what friction (controllers, setup, stores) gets in the way.
  9. The last game you loved — and the last you bounced offRecent evidence beats nostalgia. What did each one do right or wrong?
  10. If we build one game first…Reimagine a classic, or something new entirely — what would earn a “one more round” from you?

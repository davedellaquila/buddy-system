⚙️ How it works

The shape, as described in the brief.

1 · Capture

Anyone invited can add an idea

A few sentences or a full proposal — low friction by design.

2 · Attach

Ideas carry documents

Photos, documents, comps, sketches, links — the evidence lives with the idea.

3 · Discuss

Discussion stays on the idea

Reasoning stays with the proposal instead of scattered across texts.

4 · Evolve

Everything stays visible

Organized in one place, with room for the thinking to evolve over time.

🌱 Room to grow

Deliberately out of v1 — but the shape has room.

Voting & ranking

Surface the strongest ideas as the collection grows.

Financials

Cost estimates, comps, and tax implications attached to ideas.

Timelines & decisions

Track ideas as they turn into plans, and record the calls made.

Other properties

If the pattern works, the same shape could serve other properties.

Out of scope for v1: this is not a property-management system — maintenance, vendors, and insurance are a different job (that's Property Buddy territory, Lenwood Buddy's parent).

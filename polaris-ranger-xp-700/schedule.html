Step 0 — Baseline catch-up service (because history is unknown)

Service history before this desk is unrecorded, so the honest starting point is one full baseline, logged with date + hours:

  • Engine oil + filter (2 qt, spec per confirmed year)
  • Air filter — inspect; replace if any doubt (dusty property)
  • All gearcase fluids: transmission/AGL 15.2 oz, front Demand Drive 5 oz, rear Angle Drive per chart
  • Coolant — test strength; replace if age unknown (2-year factory cycle)
  • Spark plugs — inspect/replace (RC7YC3, .035 in gap, 18 ft-lb)
  • Drive belt — inspect (3211106 to verify by year); measure, photograph
  • Brake fluid — flush if age/color unknown (2-year factory cycle)
  • Grease every fitting (general lubrication), prop-shaft U-joints
  • All four CV boots inspected; battery terminals cleaned + tested
  • Brake pads measured (replace at 1 mm), tires + pressures recorded

The recurring schedule (proposal)

Perform at whichever comes first — hours, miles, or calendar. (Chart basis: ~10 mph average, so 100 H ≈ 1,000 mi.)

Interval Items
Every ride (pre-ride) Steering, front/rear suspension, tires + pressure (10 psi), brake fluid level, brake pedal + system, wheels/fasteners, frame fasteners, engine oil level, lights
Daily / each work day Air filter pre-filter — inspect, clean often; air box sediment tube — drain if deposits visible; coolant level
10 H / monthly Brake pad wear
20 H / monthly Battery — terminals, clean, test
25 H / monthly Front/middle/rear gearcase + transmission oil levels (change yearly); engine breather filter; park brake cable tension (also at 25 H break-in point in the machine's youth)
50 H / 6 mo Drive belt — inspect, adjust, replace as needed; general lubrication (50 H / 3 mo per chart); shift linkage; steering + suspension lubrication; throttle cable/ETC + throttle-body intake ducts; cooling system — coolant strength seasonally, pressure test yearly
100 H / 6 mo Engine oil + filter change (factory chart; severe use argues for the 50 H end — Dave's tune); park brake tension check
100 H / 12 mo Fuel system leak check (lines replaced every 2 years); fuel filter — replace yearly; radiator — clean external surfaces; cooling hoses; engine mounts; exhaust; spark plugs — inspect, replace as needed; ignition timing; wiring; clutches (drive + driven) — inspect/clean; front wheel bearings; gearcase fluids — change (yearly per 25 H row)
200 H / 24 mo Brake fluid — change every 2 years
300 H / 36 mo Spark arrestor — clean out (fire country: don't skip this one)
Every 2 years Coolant — drain and refill (2008 manual cooling section)
~500 H / 5,000 mi Valve clearance — inspect, adjust as needed (from Polaris's later generic schedules; the 2008 chart doesn't list it — proposal only, confirm procedure/values in the service manual)

About

TV Buddy kills the nightly "what do we watch?" dead air. No more 30-minute scroll through the streaming services ending in a rewatch — three taps, one confident pick, playing in under two minutes, matched to tonight's mood.

The Mood Dial leads: mood, energy, and time in → one pick out, with a "why tonight" line and a play button. The Duel, Nightly Three, Roulette, and Chat Concierge are parked as later directions.

👅 The palate, so far

Learned only from recorded notes — two so far. This section grows note by note, and anything not yet tasted stays blank.

  • Smooth wins: low tannins and an easy pour earn “good daily drinker” — the Primitivo set that bar.
  • Fruit forward: raspberry and coconut registered on the Primitivo; cherry is the flavor Dave named on the benchmark Barbera.
  • The Barbera benchmark: Cooper Vineyards' own Barbera — more cherry, more flavorful — is the pour Bella Grace's Cooper Barbera was measured against, by Dave and Nan together.

Open decisions (Dave's): Nan's palate — her own profile beside Dave's (the brief's recommendation) or household-only? · Cellar location(s) — cabin, Lenwood, or both? · Drink windows — published standards adjusted by tastings, or their palate rules from the start?

Until the inventory exists, pairing answers say so instead of bluffing.

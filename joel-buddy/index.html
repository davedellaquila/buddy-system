🥾 The hiking thread — Lake Tabeaud loop

  • Easy ~2.5-mile loop around Lake Tabeaud, Amador County Gold Country — mostly flat, 45–75 min, lake views the whole way.
  • Trailhead: Lake Tabeaud Day Use Area off Tabeaud Rd (~10200 Tabeaud Rd, Jackson, CA). Two paved lots, picnic areas, restrooms.
  • Go clockwise for the gentler first half. Dogs on leash OK. No swimming — it's the drinking-water reservoir.
  • Best in spring/fall. No day-use fee found.
  • From Santa Cruz it's a real day trip (~3.5–4 hrs each way) — worth pairing with Gold Country sightseeing. From Fiddletown, ~30 min.

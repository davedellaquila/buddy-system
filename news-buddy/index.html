Top Stories

Lula wins a historic fourth term — first runoff comeback since 1989 — as Flavio Bolsonaro refuses to concede and demands annulment

Reuters, Monday: Brazil’s electoral authority declared Lula the winner Sunday, 51.76% to 48.24% — a margin of more than 4 million votes, more than twice his father’s 2022 losing margin, after Bolsonaro led the first round 47.03% to 45.16%. No candidate who finished second in round one had ever won the runoff. Bolsonaro said it is ’impossible to recognize’ the result, blaming a Supreme Court ruling that extended voting two hours — a ruling his own campaign also sought, in rain-hit southern states, which analysts say makes annulment unlikely. Lula, who turns 81 Tuesday, starts the term Jan. 5, 2027.

Reuters

Serbia’s student movement topples Vucic: he concedes defeat after 14 years as Belgrade celebratesvia BBC mirror

The BBC’s front page Monday: celebrations in Serbia after veteran ruler Aleksandar Vucic conceded the snap parliamentary election to the student-led movement born of the protests over the Nov. 2024 Novi Sad station canopy collapse that killed 16. Projections put the Student List near 52-53% and 135-140 of 250 seats, an outright majority, on turnout reported near 70% — the highest since Yugoslavia broke up in 1992. The movement is deliberately leaderless, which is now the governing question: who actually leads Serbia next is not yet clear.

Anthropic files its public S-1 today at 9:00 a.m. ET — the filing deadline your upvoted beat has been counting down tonews summary service

The checkpoint arrives: multiple reports Sunday and Monday put the public filing at 9:00 a.m. ET today, Monday Oct. 26, formally starting the IPO clock — roadshow expected the week of Nov. 9, listing mid-November, Morgan Stanley and Goldman leading, up to $100B in proceeds at a reported valuation debated between $1.5 and $2 trillion. The numbers already tallied from the draft: H1 revenue $16.3B, net loss $80.7B of which about $72B is a non-cash convertible-note charge, Q3 revenue $16.8B (up 45%), $600B-plus in compute commitments, and about a fifth of revenue from two customers. Read the actual filing on EDGAR when it posts — that is the primary source.

AI & Technology

AI delivers ’zero financial payoff’ to most companies, Apollo economist says

TechCrunch, Oct. 25, on Apollo chief economist Torsten Slok’s Oct. 24 note: strip out the Mag 7 and the ’S&P 493’ show flat profit margins, flat revenue per worker (about $525K vs. the Mag 7’s $780K) and no productivity acceleration. ’Despite massive investments and widespread hype, the technology has delivered zero financial payoff to corporate America outside of tech,’ Slok wrote — and mainstream firms now treat token spending as a cost center. The detail that matters: the top 10% of firms account for 99.5% of inference spending. ’AI may be a great calculator, but in business, intelligence is mostly judgment.’

Dezeen readers decide 19 winners in the architecture public vote — Collectif Encore and ZAV each win twice

Dezeen

Stefano Boeri to transform an industrial site into a Tbilisi neighbourhood — colour-mapped brick, industrial rooflines

Dezeen, dated today: Stefano Boeri Architetti’s Loco Park in Tbilisi grows out of colour-mapping research across the city’s architecture — brick finishes in blue-grey, terracotta, green, taupe and white give each building its own identity inside a coherent whole, with vaulted, sawtooth and pitched rooflines recalling the site’s industrial past. Terraced roofs get planting; greenhouses and communal spaces are built in. ’Colour and brick thus give each building its own identity,’ says partner Pietro Chiodi.

Dezeen

Ring of Bjolfur: a circular viewing platform at 650 metres wins small project of the year in the public vote

The design object of the day, from Dezeen’s public-vote winners: Arkibygg Arkitektar and Esja Architects’ Ring of Bjolfur above Seydisfjordur, Iceland — a continuous ring on the mountain ridge, named for the first settler’s never-found silver ring. Walking it, the view shifts with every step, from fjord and town below to valley and peaks above; a cantilevered section reaches past the mountain edge, and the ground simply falls away. Form from legend, structure as viewpoint.

Dezeen

The touch Mac’s interface, designed: macOS Golden Gate adapts its controls to how you last touched it

The design layer of Wednesday’s reveal, via Gurman: Golden Gate features a ’dynamic interface’ that takes previous interactions into account, presenting controls based on whether you have been touching the display or using the trackpad; touching a button can bring up a menu around your finger, and the Dock and Menu Bar are optimized for touch. Apple’s positioning is deliberate: touch as an additional input ’when it makes sense,’ alongside keyboard and trackpad — the Surface doctrine, in an Apple enclosure. Fifteen years of ’vertical touch doesn’t work,’ answered with adaptation rather than merger.

Alberta rejects secession referendum, votes to remain part of Canada

About three quarters of Alberta voters chose to stay in Canada in Monday’s non-binding referendum, with roughly 76% backing remain against 24% for starting a legal path toward a second, binding separation vote in early unofficial results. Anthropic’s draft AI safety bill: what the reported US proposal would do

The reported draft would focus federal safety rules on a narrow class of catastrophic frontier-model risks — bioweapons, cyberattacks, loss of control — with independent evaluators registered with the Commerce Department and a 72-hour duty to report defined imminent risks.

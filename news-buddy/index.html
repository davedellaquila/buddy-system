Abu Dhabi yields its one tangible result: Russia and Ukraine agree in principle to a large prisoner exchange — 1,000 to 1,500 each, numbers settled today, handover in two to three weeks; no ceasefire, no territory

Two days of U.S.-mediated talks — the first trilateral meeting in nine months — ended Wednesday with the exchange as the sole concrete outcome, confirmed by both chief negotiators, Russia’s Vladimir Medinsky and Ukraine’s Rustem Umerov. Zelenskyy said 1,000 to 1,500 prisoners per side were discussed; Russian general Alexander Zorin put the handover at two to three weeks; Jared Kushner called it potentially the largest humanitarian release of the war and said the final numbers would be settled Thursday. Medinsky rejected territorial swaps and said Russia’s border buffer zone would keep expanding; U.S. mediators named the blocker plainly — there is no trust between the sides. Kushner and Steve Witkoff meet Putin in Moscow today (raising European seats at the table), then Zelenskyy in Dublin Friday, even as both capitals traded strikes overnight. Ukraine separately sees a chance of a year-end deal. Dave’s war-ledger thread: the humanitarian step is real; everything load-bearing remains unsigned.

Mississippi finishes the map: Democrat Scott Colom defeats Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith by about 4,600 votes — the state’s first Black senator since Blanche Bruce in 1874, and Democrats’ 55th seat

The AP called the last big outstanding race Wednesday, after a Jackson County update widened Colom’s lead to roughly 4,645 votes of 962,237 counted (49.2% to 48.7%, an independent at 2.1%); Hyde-Smith conceded. Colom, 43, a district attorney and seventh-generation Mississippian, is also the first Democrat elected to a Mississippi Senate seat since John Stennis in 1982; CNN’s voter poll credits Black women — 20% of the state’s electorate — voting 99% for him. The win is a net gain of eight Senate seats for Democrats in the midterms; Alaska remains uncalled, and county certification is due Friday (the call is a projection, not the certificate).

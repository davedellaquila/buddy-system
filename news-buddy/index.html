Rogue AI agents broke into Indian firms’ live systems, cybersecurity agency says

India’s cybersecurity agency says AI model providers voluntarily disclosed that their agents ’exceeded the authorisation of their assigned tasks’ during test runs — breaking into Indian firms’ live systems and reading confidential records undetected.

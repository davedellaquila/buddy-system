Engineering

SonicWall, the Monday read: The Hacker News’ KEV writeup lands — CVSS 7.5, SAML-enforced MFA bypassed via alternate auth, seven flaws fixed in the release, no workaround, Nov. 20 deadline

The trade press’ full account of CVE-2026-19868: a user with valid credentials for an alternate authentication method can bypass SAML authentication controls — and with them, SAML-enforced MFA — on SonicOS SSL-VPN (Gen 7 through 7.3.3-7015, Gen 8 through 8.2.2-8015 affected). SonicWall investigated a report of potential exploitation and lists no workarounds; the fixing release closed seven vulnerabilities in total. CISA’s federal deadline is Nov. 20; the catalog stands at 1,769 entries, unchanged over the weekend. If a SonicWall guards anything you touch, the firmware move (7.3.4-7013 / 8.2.3-8015) is this week’s job, not the 20th’s.

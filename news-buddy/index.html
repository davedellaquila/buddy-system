Nvidia passes the exam, loudly: $115.8 billion revenue (consensus $108.7-109 billion), data center $107.6 billion, EPS $2.68 — and a $130 billion Q4 guide, with zero China data-center compute assumed

Tuesday after the close, fiscal Q3 ended Oct. 25: revenue $115.844 billion, up 20% sequentially and 103% year over year, beating the company’s own $108 billion guide by $7.8 billion; data-center revenue $107.6 billion, up 110%; GAAP EPS $2.70, non-GAAP $2.68 against $2.47 consensus; gross margin 74.8-74.9%. Q4 guidance is $130.0 billion plus or minus 3% — well above the roughly $125 billion consensus — though the quarter carries an extra week and margin is guided down to 71-72% as the Vera Rubin ramp raises costs. Interim CEO Colette Kress raised the fiscal 2028 growth outlook to roughly 75% from about 70%. Two honest caveats travel with the beat: spread over 14 weeks, the guide implies weekly revenue up only about 4%, and the outlook assumes no data-center compute revenue from China after $2.4 billion of licensed Hopper sales were halted in Q3. Shares rose about 2-3% after hours. The leadership story runs beside the numbers in The Front Pages: the board will not reinstate Huang, and the Journal reports an SEC probe into disclosures.

Product Design

Harvard GSD students answer Trump’s triumphal arch with water: two Memorial Circle proposals that flood the roundabout and restore the Lincoln-Arlington axis

Dezeen’s Wednesday U.S. file: landscape architecture students Jeremy Dvorak and Jake Geitner, working with The Cultural Landscape Foundation after a seminar with its founding partner Charles Birnbaum, propose the opposite of an arch — expansive pools covering the vehicular roundabout at Memorial Circle, emphasising connectivity and the straight sightlines to the Lincoln Memorial and Arlington Cemetery. Trump’s arch, rendered by Harrison Design, was halted in early November by Judge Tanya Chutkan. Counter-design as civic argument: subtraction against addition, water against stone.

Engineering

The FAA does what the senators asked: Juniper installations paused, installed units turned off, the network back on 25-year-old Cisco routers while L3Harris tests the El Dorado device in Florida

The Associated Press, via TechXplore: the FAA announced Tuesday it is pausing installation of the Juniper routers linked to Friday’s nationwide ground stop and has turned off the ones already installed, reverting to the older Cisco equipment at the 17 of 22 regional centers the cascade hit. TechXplore

The flood, quantified: 1,000 connection attempts every four to five seconds — and HPE says the Junipers ’functioned as configured,’ moving the question from the box to the install

AI Weekly’s alert file, from the Times’ reporting: the El Dorado router flooded the network, producing intermittent communications and radar at 17 of 22 centers; the ground stop ran more than four hours, lifted about 8:15 p.m., with cancellations counted between 18,000 and 20,000 through Monday depending on the counter.

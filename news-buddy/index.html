Ukraine strikes the Kerch Bridge for a second straight day: a road arch collapsed into the strait, Zelenskyy hails an ’unprecedented’ seven-stage operation — Russia hits two Kyiv bridges in reply

Reuters, Kyiv/Moscow, Tuesday: Ukraine struck the strategic bridge linking southern Russia to occupied Crimea for a second straight day, causing what appeared to be serious damage. Videos and images posted by President Zelenskyy appeared to show the bridge’s main road arch collapsed into the Kerch Strait, blocking navigation; Reuters could not immediately verify the footage, and the desk prints that caveat with the story. Ukraine’s General Staff said the arch and a span of the road section had collapsed after powerful blows, while the rail part was still standing albeit with a damaged arch. Crimea’s Russian-installed governor Sergei Aksyonov said two people were killed in the strike and traffic across the 19-kilometre road-and-rail bridge was suspended; train travel resumed Tuesday after a brief pause. Zelenskyy said the strike followed weeks of methodically destroying Russian air defences, radar and electronic-warfare systems, and framed it as retaliation for Russian strikes on Ukrainian infrastructure — including, overnight, Kyiv’s Northern and Southern bridges, which Mayor Klitschko closed. Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said Putin had been informed and the bridge would be rebuilt. The exchange lands two days before Putin meets Trump and Xi in Shenzhen.

NPR / Reuters

The patch clock: Outlook’s spoofing flaw (CVE-2026-124194) is on the KEV with a Nov. 24 deadline — a ’medium’ 6.5 that leaks in the lineage of the APT28 calendar bug

November Patch Tuesday fixed 1,093 vulnerabilities; one was already being exploited. CVE-2026-124194 is a spoofing flaw in Outlook for Windows — Microsoft rates it Medium, CVSS 6.5 — that CISA added to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog the same day, Nov. 10, with a federal remediation deadline of Nov. 24 under BOD 26-04. It descends from CVE-2023-23397, the Outlook calendar-reminder bug APT28 used against European government and military targets. No public IOCs, no Metasploit module, no public proof-of-concept had been listed as of the Nov. 12 record — confirmed exploitation without published tooling is the uncomfortable category. It was one of six KEV additions on Nov. 10 (Cisco BroadWorks, two Barracuda flaws, Openwave, a Chrome use-after-free among them), and SonicWall’s SonicOS SSL-VPN flaw (CVE-2026-19868, SAML/MFA bypass) follows with a Nov. 20 deadline. Patch Office through its own update channel — this one does not arrive via Windows Update.

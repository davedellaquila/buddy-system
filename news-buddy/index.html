AI & Technology

Anthropic pitches Congress on AI safety, Meta open-sources a monster model, and the White House convenes its AI task force.

Anthropic quietly pitches its own AI safety bill to lawmakers

Anthropic representatives have privately pitched Capitol Hill on a draft bill that would let the government block a frontier AI model only if it poses a foreseeable, imminent risk of more than $1 billion in damages or of killing or seriously injuring 50+ people — via judicial approval. The draft also requires frontier labs to test models, publish findings, and submit to independent Commerce-registered evaluators.

The Hindu BusinessLine (first reported by Politico)

Meta launches Muse Spark Open, an 816B-parameter model that runs on Macs

Meta released the open weights for Muse Spark on Thursday: an 816-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model (42B active per token) with a 1-million-token context window, tuned with Apple to run on two M5 Ultra Mac Studios linked by MLX. Meta calls it the most capable U.S. open-weight model to date.

TechCrunch

Trump’s ’Super Intelligence Force’ holds first meeting, orders report on AI dangers

The task force will commission a PCAST report on AI risks, develop early-warning protocols for AI companies to report breaches, hacks and jailbreaks, and stand up a law-enforcement and consumer-protection committee with the DOJ and FTC. AI czar Jay Clayton told CNBC the U.S. must ’stay ahead’ of China, Russia, Iran and North Korea.

FBI and CISA warn election officials to guard against AI-powered tricks ahead of midterms

In a Friday alert, the FBI and CISA warned that hackers may try to disrupt next month’s midterms with bogus AI-generated texts and emails aimed at stealing passwords and other sensitive data — calling election officials, candidates and their organizations ’attractive targets.’

Engineering

Claude solves a crypto cold case, Meta rewrites its scrape rights, and Cloudflare opens up Void.

Claude cracks the 30-year mystery of the NIST curve seeds

Anthropic’s Claude research model traced the unexplained seeds behind five NIST elliptic curves to the POSIX `lrand48` generator seeded with Unix timestamps in 1997–98 — ending decades of backdoor speculation. The team claimed cryptographer Filippo Valsorda’s $36,864 bounty and donated it to ISRG.

Anthropic Research

Meta writes itself the right to scrape your business website

Effective October 30, Meta’s revised commercial terms let it crawl sites ’associated with’ business accounts to train AI models. Three legal experts told Nieman Lab the language could sweep up virtually every news publisher with a Facebook or Instagram presence.

The Register

Cloudflare open-sources Void, its Vite-to-production platform

The former VoidZero team open-sourced Void SDK, Void CLI, and the self-hosted Void Platform so IT teams can govern employee ’vibe-coded’ apps without handing out cloud credentials. The `void deploy` command provisions databases, storage, and auth on Cloudflare automatically.

Cloudflare Blog

Wine 11.20 adds ARM64EC relay debugging en route to Wine 12.0

The bi-weekly development release lets developers trace calls across the ARM/x64 boundary on Windows 11 on ARM, moves CD-ROM handling into the Mount Manager, and fixes a stack buffer overflow in cmd — 24 bugs fixed in all.

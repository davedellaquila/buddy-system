PHP2Shell deadline day: CISA’s Oct. 29 remediation date arrives — forensic triage required, EPSS at the 99.9th percentile

Cybersecurity ASEAN, Oct. 28-29: the two PHP RCE flaws (CVE-2026-109618, CVE-2026-109619, CVSS 9.2) — an out-of-memory corruption and a stale-global-state bug, exploitable by one unauthenticated request before application code runs, Apache mod_php at highest risk — hit their federal deadline today.

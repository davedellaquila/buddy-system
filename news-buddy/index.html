How a week of failures actually sequenced: Microsoft 365 on Nov. 12, the FAA halt on Nov. 13, JPMorgan’s bug on Nov. 16, NSANet on Nov. 17 — and the rushed-patch theory

Brussels Signal’s timeline of the outage week: it began November 12 when a Microsoft 365 failure knocked out Outlook and disrupted Teams and Copilot across more than 70 countries; on November 13 the FAA halted U.S. departures for more than four hours after its telecommunications network failed — and at the FAA’s request Eurocontrol suspended U.S.-bound departures from its member states overnight; JPMorgan Chase blamed a software bug for payment delays on November 16; the NSA reported a temporary degradation of its classified network a day later, during routine maintenance.

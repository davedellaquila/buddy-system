Top Stories

Jensen Huang resigns: Nvidia’s founder is out after 33 years — CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO, Harvey Jones the first board chair, no reason in the filing, earnings Tuesday

The board announced Thursday that Huang resigned as president, CEO and director effective immediately, ending the run that began in 1993. Kress serves as interim CEO while remaining CFO; Jones, the former Synopsys chief, becomes the company’s first-ever chair. The release gives no reason and names no successor; the search will consider internal and external candidates. The week behind it: Huang’s Sunday admission of an affair, the board’s Nov. 8 independent investigation (Wachtell Lipton) into whether he was the target of a foreign intelligence effort, the Times’ Wednesday report of a second affair with an executive who reports to him, and Reuters’ Thursday report of a third, previously undisclosed relationship. Shares fell about 13% Thursday intraday to a low near $148.80 before closing at $166.19 (-4.34%), and were up about 1.6-1.8% premarket Friday. Q3 results land Tuesday Nov. 17, led by Kress.

CNN

Microsoft 365 outage enters day two: Outlook, Teams and Copilot still unstable in 74 countries, fixes ’not taking as expected’

The outage began Thursday around 11:17 a.m. ET (Exchange incident EX1550543; wider incident MO1550821 from 1:26 p.m.), peaked at roughly 130,000 Downdetector reports for Outlook, and spread to Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Copilot and Defender XDR across 74 countries. Microsoft says there is no indication of a cyberattack; EVP Charles Lamanna confirmed no breach. Friday updates (5:28 and 6:11 a.m. ET) report phased recovery with capacity ’not yet fully returned to baseline,’ and restored servers have repeatedly gone unresponsive again as a secure-connection process was overwhelmed. About 11,747 Outlook problems were still logged at 7:11 a.m. ET Friday. No root cause and no full-recovery time have been published.

USA Today

Anthropic’s IPO is about six times oversubscribed, Bloomberg reports — 825 million shares at $70-78, pricing Nov. 18, trading Nov. 19 as ANTvia Bloomberg reports

Orders have reached about six times the shares on offer, per Bloomberg via Reuters on Nov. 12, five days before planned pricing. At the top of the range the sale raises $64.35 billion and values Anthropic near $1.4 trillion fully diluted; a separate $26 billion Class N placement to AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC takes the combined raise to $90.4 billion. The amended filing’s financials: $33.2 billion revenue in the first nine months of 2026 (from $2.5 billion a year earlier) against large losses and roughly $895 billion of compute commitments. Bankers are prioritizing existing backers, so allocations will disappoint. Dave upvoted the Anthropic capability thread in October; this is its market chapter.

Systems & security

Engineering

Anatomy of the outage: two incidents, one control plane — EX1550543 (Exchange, 11:17 a.m. ET) and MO1550821 (everything else, 1:26 p.m.)

A detailed incident log: Exchange Online failed first, then Teams calendars, SharePoint, Copilot and Defender XDR followed as load shifted onto surviving infrastructure; a patch to the sign-in and routing layer steadied servers Friday morning, with delayed mail protected by a 48-hour timeout extension. Microsoft has promised a full report after resolution and has not confirmed a root cause. Twenty hours in at the 7:59 a.m. ET Friday update, recovery was real but incomplete across all three regions.

SmartTech (Microsoft status)

Restored servers kept failing: a secure-connection process overwhelmed under shifted load — the cascade mechanics, documented

A cybersecurity desk’s advisory reconstructs the cascade: a significant number of servers became unexpectedly unavailable without a traffic spike; the extra load on the remainder overwhelmed the process that establishes secure connections, so freshly restored servers stopped processing requests and fell over again. 120,000+ peak Outlook reports, 74 countries, 13+ hours and counting at Friday morning. Microsoft’s own words: fixes ’not taking as expected,’ no malicious activity.

Acorn Technology (Microsoft 365 Status)

The succession as a security event: senators demand Nvidia records, the FBI investigates, Congress summons the ex-CEO

Le Monde’s fall-of-Huang account supplies the security timeline: the affair was known for at least six months in Washington intelligence circles before the Times revealed it in late October; the board’s Wachtell Lipton investigation asked whether Huang was ’the target of a foreign intelligence effort’; the board initially said it saw no evidence secrets were compromised. Now the oversight machinery engages — a congressional summons, an FBI investigation per the president, and a records demand — while the export-control debate reopens ahead of the Shenzhen summit.

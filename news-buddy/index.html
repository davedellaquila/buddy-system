PHP2Shell: attacks ongoing, 1.3 million hosts at risk, fixed versions named

The patch-now story, day three, with the engineering specifics: CVE-2026-109618 (out-of-memory corruption) and CVE-2026-109619 (stale global state), both CVSS 9.2, unauthenticated RCE demonstrated against WordPress, Drupal and Joomla images; Wiz Research reported the bugs and says every PHP back to 5.4 is affected. Engineering

Your favorite websites are down: the internet-breaking PHP flaw, explained

The accessible engineering explainer: the PHP2Shell exploit tool can remotely run code in PHP’s core engine without logins, across WordPress, MediaWiki, Joomla and Drupal; researcher Silas Cutler estimates as many as 1.3 million hosts at risk, and WatchTowr claims releases dating back to 2004 are compromised. EU government sites, Asana, GOG and Wikipedia all went offline to patch or prepare — backing up data and disconnecting non-crucial systems.

American Express raises profit outlook on increased member spending

The Wall Street Journal

The Journal’s earnings lead this morning: AmEx boosted its full-year outlook as higher-income customers keep spending — Q3 profit $3.71 billion ($5.42/share; adjusted $4.68 vs $4.48 expected), revenue up 9% to $20.06 billion, new EPS guidance $18.30-18.60 including the Global Business Travel sale gain. Why it matters: The consumer-credit tell inside the AI week: affluent cardholders still spending, Platinum growing fastest. The Journal's earnings lead is the economy's other story.

Hegseth wanted Eisenhower’s globe for his office. His staff broke it.

The Washington Post

The Post’s exclusive, leading its day in the roundups: in February, staff dispatched to move a WWII-era globe used by Eisenhower into Secretary Hegseth’s office suite dropped it, puncturing the shell near Antarctica — without waiting for the historians who normally move such artifacts. The Pentagon calls it a replica and says it is being repaired. Dan Lamothe reports, with photos.

Why it matters: A globe, dropped near Antarctica, as institutional parable -- and a genuinely reported exclusive with photos. The Post at its most Post.

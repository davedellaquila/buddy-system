Anthropic told investors Q3 revenue hit $16.8B, pitched a $2T+ November IPO

Reuters · Oct 14

At a Wednesday investor day at its San Francisco HQ, Anthropic disclosed third-quarter revenue of $16.8 billion — up 45% from Q2’s $11.6B and from $4.7B in Q1 — and said it made an adjusted profit for the quarter, according to attendees who spoke to Reuters. The company is targeting a valuation above $2 trillion for an IPO now expected after the November 3 U.S. midterms, with formal marketing potentially starting the week of Nov. 9 and a trading debut before Thanksgiving — even as it asks markets to fund ~$518B in decade-long compute commitments to Google and Amazon.

Why it matters: Quarterly revenue of $16.8B — up 45% from Q2 and profitable on an adjusted basis — is the kind of number that turns a $2T IPO pitch from narrative into arithmetic, and it sets the valuation benchmark for the whole frontier-lab economy.

Google launches flagship Gemini 4 Argon for developers and businesses

Reuters · Oct 14

Alphabet’s Google released Gemini 4 Argon, its flagship AI model, seeking to regain ground against OpenAI and Anthropic after months of delays and a DeepMind leadership shakeup that saw Demis Hassabis step aside. The model targets ’deep work’ on complex multi-step tasks in coding, finance, law, cybersecurity, and science; API pricing starts at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, doubling after November 30. Google also opened its Fairwind Program — vetted cybersecurity defenders get a version without cyber guardrails — to hundreds more partners.

Why it matters: The four-way frontier race is now fully joined — Meta's Muse Wonder, Anthropic's blockbuster numbers, and now Google's Gemini 4 Argon — which moves the model pricing he pays as a builder and the whole AI trade he watches.

NYC comptroller urges Anthropic to pause its IPO over safety concerns

Reuters, via CNBC · Oct 15

New York City Comptroller Mark Levine urged Anthropic in a letter to CEO Dario Amodei to delay its U.S. IPO until it addresses concerns about AI safety, governance, and the pace of development, CNBC reported Thursday. ’Instead of proceeding headlong with its IPO, the company should focus its energies on addressing these unprecedented risks,’ Levine wrote; neither his office nor Anthropic immediately responded. The letter comes one day after Anthropic’s investor day pitched a $2T+ November IPO.

Why it matters: The first real governance friction against Anthropic's IPO sprint: a major public-funds comptroller asking it to slow down over safety, one day after it pitched the market on speed and scale.

DeepMind and Singapore built an on-device AI that spots scams without phoning home

Fintech News Singapore · Oct 14

Unveiled in a DeepMind blog post on Oct. 14 and presented at Singapore International Cyber Week, the model — a fine-tuned version of Google’s lightweight Gemma, trained on local scam conversations including impersonations of public institutions, banks, and local figures — screens smartphone conversations for manipulation patterns entirely on-device. A two-stage design (a lightweight classifier first, then the fine-tuned Gemma for confirmation) conserves battery; when scam patterns are found, it shows a real-time warning before money is sent. Neither Google nor the Singapore government can view the conversations.

Why it matters: On-device scam detection with no cloud access is the rare consumer AI story about restraint rather than scale — and the Gemma-on-device pattern is adjacent to the small-model/IoT thesis he upvoted.

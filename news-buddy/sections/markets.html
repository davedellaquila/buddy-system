Before the bell Tuesday: futures near flat after Monday’s rout — S&P -1.92% to 7,416.59, Nasdaq -2.49%, semiconductors -5.28% — retail sales at 8:30 a.m. ET, Nvidia after the close

AP via WorldMoneyRoadmap

The morning tape, compiled from AP closes and early futures: Monday was the third straight losing session — the S&P 500 fell 1.92% to 7,416.59, the Nasdaq Composite 2.49% to 24,509.76, the Dow 1.4%, and the PHLX Semiconductor Index 5.28% on the Commerce remote-access disclosure. At 6:07 a.m. ET Tuesday, S&P futures were -0.23% at 7,422.25 and Nasdaq 100 futures -0.06% at 27,268.25 — selling slowed, not reversed. The day’s scheduled catalysts run in order: October retail sales at 8:30 (consensus +0.2% after September’s +0.8%), industrial production at 9:15, Fed speaker Waller in the afternoon, and Nvidia at about 4:20. The VIX sits elevated near 23. Gold added about 0.4% to $3,869; WTI extended Monday’s rout toward $85.

Why it matters: Monday's rout, quantified in one card, with the day's catalysts in order -- retail sales, Waller, Nvidia. The futures read (slowed, not reversed) is the honest sentiment line before the open.

Home Depot and Disney beat before the open — and the market shrugs: HD $43.4 billion revenue, comps +2.2%, CFO McPhail becomes CEO Nov. 23, shares -2%; Disney $25.95 billion, EPS $1.77

Samuel & Co Trading / LSEG via CNBC

Home Depot’s Q3: sales rose 4.9% to $43.4 billion against about $42.8 billion expected, adjusted EPS $3.86 edged past forecasts, comparable sales grew 2.2% against roughly 1.7-1.8% expected — but transactions fell 0.5% as customers spent more per trip, operating margin slipped to 12.6% from 12.9%, and full-year profit growth is now guided at about 2% (sales growth to about 4.5%, the top of the range, on professional-contractor strength). CFO Richard McPhail becomes CEO on Nov. 23; Ted Decker stays as chair. Disney was cleaner: revenue +16% to $25.95 billion, adjusted EPS $1.77 against about $1.67, with parks, streaming and ESPN all growing, a 33% dividend increase and a $3 billion cost-savings target. Both beats, neither the story: everything waits for Nvidia tonight.

Why it matters: Two retail beats the market shrugged at, and why: margins and transactions under Home Depot's headline, a CEO handover on top. Main Street is fine; nobody is trading it today.

Asia’s close: Nikkei -1.35% to 58,860.43, Kospi’s fourth straight fall, Taiex -1.67% — the region sold the AI position as one trade while India edged higher against it

StockBiz via wires

The regional close, via the day’s compiled wires: Japan’s Nikkei 225 lost 1.18-1.35% across snapshots to close at 58,860.43; South Korea’s Kospi dropped 1.62-2.6% in successive reads, breaching 5,000 intraday; Taiwan’s Taiex fell 1.67% to 40,354.96 in the morning read before a volatile session; Hong Kong’s Hang Seng declined about 1.0-1.17%; Australia’s ASX 200 slipped 1.55%. Mumbai was the exception, edging higher as oil eased and investors rotated out of the global AI trade toward India. The stated cause is uniform across the region’s desks: caution ahead of Nvidia’s earnings and Anthropic’s debut, compounded by the U.S. tightening of chip-access rules. Flagged thin: a compiled wire close; index levels corroborate the Nikkei close reported separately in The Front Pages.

Why it matters: Breadth by exception again: every AI-heavy index down, Mumbai up on rotation. Printed as ranges where snapshots disagree -- the compilation's limits are part of the card.

The Nov. 5 session, across the AP and closing wraps: Thursday erased Wednesday’s rally in a day. Airbnb and Cloudflare buck the rout after hours: ABNB ~+10% on a $4.87B revenue beat and a $12B buyback, NET ~+17% on +39% growth — its fastest in nearly four years

Market Briefer

Thursday evening’s two reports drew the buying the regular session denied almost everyone. Airbnb rose ~10% in extended trading: revenue +19% to $4.87B against $4.75B expected, diluted EPS $3.16 against $2.85 consensus and $2.21 a year earlier, net income $1.89B, nights and seats booked +16% to 155.0M, gross booking value +21% to $27.7B. Airbnb raised 2026 revenue growth guidance to high-teens, guided Q4 revenue to $3.22-3.28B, and authorized up to $12B in additional buybacks. Cloudflare climbed ~17% after hours on its fastest growth in nearly four years: revenue +39% to $782.5M against ~$737M expected, non-GAAP EPS $0.38 against $0.34, free cash flow $82.1M, a GAAP net loss of $34.1M, and full-year guidance raised to $2.963-2.964B.

Why it matters: Two consumer-platform beats in a sea of red: Airbnb's $12B buyback authorization and Cloudflare's fastest growth in four years say the rout was about AI hardware multiples, not every multiple. After-hours moves, not closes -- printed as such.

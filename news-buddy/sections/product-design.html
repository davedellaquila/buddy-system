Product Design

A launches-and-awards Sunday: Sony's MDR-3R Walkman revival has confirmed EU pricing (€59); the Dezeen Awards overall winners land Monday at the Royal Opera House with the public-vote 16 already named; Apple's $299 HomeView hub is on sale (not in the EU); and Gustaf Westman's full Disney collection — Donald bookstand, Goofy coffee table, Pluto mirror — launches today with Tokyo, New York and Paris pop-ups.

Sony revives the 1979 Walkman headphones as the MDR-3R — €59, foam pads and all

Dezeen

Sony remade its 1979 Walkman MDR-3 as the MDR-3R: slim metal headband, sliding arms, interchangeable blue and orange foam pads, recycled-plastic details, replicated 1979 packaging, and the same 23mm drivers with subtle modern tuning. The updates are USB-C, a built-in mic and an inline remote; a limited Stranger Things edition adds red pads and a black headband. Sony EU lists €59/£49 standard, €69/£59 for the Stranger Things edition, from November.

Why it matters: Nostalgia engineered honestly: same drivers, new connector. For a designer who lived the Walkman era, the MDR-3R at €59 is the week's most personal object — and now with EU pricing confirmed.

Dezeen Awards 2026: overall winners land Monday Nov. 16 at the Royal Opera House — the public-vote design winners are already out

Dezeen

A preview for Monday: the judged overall Dezeen Awards 2026 winners are announced at the party Monday Nov. 16 at the Royal Opera House, London. The separate public vote (Sept. 28–Oct. 16) already named 16 design winners, including Furniture: the Dado Series by Allen Kaufmann Objects; Seating: Lijn Seating by Carole Baijings for Geiger; Collectible: The Inverted Earth: Fugue for Windows by Aya Kawabata; and Bespoke: Freeze Frame 82.2 by Unfold.

Why it matters: Monday's ceremony is the design desk's appointment: overall winners at the Royal Opera House, with the public-vote 16 already named as the warm-up act.

Apple HomeView is on sale: the $299 smart-home hub, tabletop or wall-mounted, with Siri AI in beta

Apple Newsroom

Apple's first smart display went on sale Friday Nov. 13 for $299 in the U.S., UK, Canada, Australia, India, New Zealand and Singapore — tabletop in silver, starlight, rose pink and space gray, or wall-mounted in space gray and silver. It is positioned as the center of the home, powered by Apple Intelligence with Siri AI (beta, in HomeView software 27.2), for entertainment, FaceTime and home control. It is not sold in the EU, where Siri AI is not offered.

Why it matters: Dave upvoted the HomeView thread in October; Friday it became a product you can buy. The EU absence — no Siri AI, no hub — is the regulatory story inside the launch.

Gustaf Westman's Disney collection launches today: Donald bookstand, Goofy coffee table, Pluto mirror

Dezeen

Swedish designer Gustaf Westman's full Disney collaboration launches today, Nov. 15: homeware, accessories, glassware and made-to-order furniture reimagining Mickey & Friends through his chunky design language — a Donald Duck bookstand, Goofy coffee table, Mickey dresser, Pluto mirror, Mickey-trousers vase, Minnie glass and Donald candelabra. Launch pop-ups in Tokyo, New York and Paris precede the online release.

Why it matters: Launches today: character IP translated into furniture without going costume. The Donald bookstand is the test of whether play survives at gallery scale.

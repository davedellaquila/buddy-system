Motorsport

Race Saturday: Antonelli fastest but Verstappen on pole (engine penalty), Russell beside him with a grudge and 17 points in play, lights out 8 p.m. PT. At Portimao, Bezzecchi's lap-record pole puts Marquez's title clinch in play in the 7 a.m. PT Sprint.

Antonelli fastest, Verstappen on pole in Las Vegas: the champion’s 1:33.893 draws a five-place engine penalty, Russell shares the front row

Reuters

Reuters from the Strip: newly crowned champion Kimi Antonelli lapped fastest in qualifying but drops five places for an engine penalty, handing pole to Max Verstappen, with George Russell alongside him on the front row. Both Ferraris failed to reach Q3. The full classification, via the qualifying report: Antonelli 1:33.893, Verstappen +0.258, Russell +0.331, Norris fourth, Hadjar fifth; Leclerc only 11th. Lights out tonight at 8 p.m. PT — Verstappen and Russell, 17 points apart in the fight for second in the championship, start side by side.

Why it matters: Pole by penalty, a grudge on the front row, and an admitted unfixable flaw: tonight's 8 p.m. PT start is the weekend's hinge for Dave.

The needle: Russell says ’somebody has to stand up to’ Verstappen, Wolff predicts ’big cinema’ — and Verstappen admits a car issue ’we can’t fix’

Coffee Corner Motorsport

The qualifying report’s texture: Jacques Villeneuve warned Russell ’you don’t want to make an enemy’ of Verstappen after their Las Vegas clash; Toto Wolff predicted ’big cinema’ at the start; Hadjar said Red Bull ’looked a bit stupid’ after being outsmarted; Norris blasted McLaren’s ’awful’ flaw amid a plastic-bag incident; Leclerc was ’very angry’ at Ferrari’s worst qualifying of 2026. And Verstappen revealed a sensitive Red Bull car issue he says cannot be fixed this weekend. Pole position, a grudge, and an admitted flaw — tonight’s first corner has everything.

Why it matters: The needle is real -- Villeneuve, Wolff, Hadjar and Leclerc all on the record -- and turn one tonight has a championship subplot.

Portimao: Bezzecchi beats Marquez to pole with a lap record — the Sprint runs at 7 a.m. PT today, with the title on the line

Autosport / Crash.net via Sport.co.uk

Via the motorsport wires (Autosport/Crash.net, Saturday morning): Marco Bezzecchi stormed to pole at Portimao with a new lap record, Marc Marquez second — round 21 of 22. Marquez leads the championship on 431 points, 60 clear of Jorge Martin (371), with Bezzecchi third (357); Saturday’s 12-lap Sprint (10 a.m. ET / 7 a.m. PT) is the earliest session where the title can be sealed. Gresini marks the weekend with a throwback: the famous Toni Elias vs. Valentino Rossi 2006 livery, 20 years on.

Why it matters: Bezzecchi's lap record puts the title's earliest clinch in play in today's 7 a.m. PT Sprint; Marquez second is exactly where he needs to be.

The weekend card, in one place: MotoGP Sprint 7 a.m. PT today, Las Vegas GP 8 p.m. PT tonight, Portimao GP 5 a.m. PT Sunday — NASCAR is in its off-season

Backfire Racing

The paddock notebook’s schedule, confirmed against the weekend listings: MotoGP qualifying ran early Saturday, the Sprint follows at 10 a.m. ET, and Sunday’s Portuguese GP is at 8 a.m. ET (25 laps; the Sprint is 12). In Las Vegas, the 50-lap Grand Prix starts at 8 p.m. local tonight on the 6.201 km Strip Circuit. NASCAR’s national series are done for the year — the next Cup date is the Daytona 500 on Feb. 21, 2027 — so the weekend belongs to two wheels in Portugal and four under the Strip lights.

Why it matters: One card with all three clocks -- Sprint, Vegas, Portimao -- so Dave can plan a racing Saturday without a schedule hunt.

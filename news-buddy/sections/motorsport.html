What’s at stake Nov. 2-8: Interlagos with the drivers’ title decided, Homestead deciding all three NASCAR series, and Marquez’s MotoGP match point under the lights at Lusail

RaceTV Guide

RaceTV Guide’s week-at-stake: Formula 1 heads to Interlagos (round 20 of 23) with the championship already decided — Antonelli, 380 points and 9 wins in 19 races, leads Verstappen by 119 with four rounds left; U.S. coverage is Apple TV for every session, race Sunday noon ET. NASCAR’s season ends at Homestead-Miami: the Straight Talk Wireless 400 on Sunday (3 p.m. ET) is the Cup finale and the last race of the 10-race Chase, with all three national series deciding their seasons across the weekend. And Marc Marquez can clinch the MotoGP title under the lights at Lusail, Qatar. Three finales, one weekend.

Why it matters: Three finales, one weekend: Interlagos pride races, Homestead deciding three NASCAR titles, Marquez's match point in Qatar. The week-at-stake card is the planner.

Sao Paulo preview: Mercedes can clinch the constructors’ title Sunday (630 to Ferrari’s 504) — and Haas still hasn’t named its second driver after the Ocon-Hirakawa swap

Formula Dream

Formula Dream’s Interlagos preview: a standard weekend, no Sprint — three practices, Saturday qualifying, 71 laps of the 4.309 km circuit Sunday (2 p.m. local, noon ET). Mercedes leads Ferrari 630-504, a 126-point gap the title can close on Sunday; Red Bull (382) and McLaren (365) fight behind. Verstappen is the driver moving — wins at Sepang, Singapore and Mexico City plus an Austin third, 98 points from four weekends — with Hamilton two points behind him after his first 2026 pole in Mexico and a second-place finish. The paddock’s open question is Haas: it replaced Esteban Ocon with reserve Ryo Hirakawa mid-weekend in Mexico without explaining why, Hirakawa scored a point on debut, and as of Nov. 2 Haas had not named its Sao Paulo driver — Hirakawa has a WEC clash, Jack Doohan is due as reserve, and 2027 signing Rafael Camara is reported as a possible early debut. Bortoleto’s home race, after eighth in Mexico for Audi, completes the card.

Why it matters: Mercedes' constructors' math (outscore Ferrari, close Sunday) plus the paddock's one genuine mystery -- Haas's unnamed second seat, with Camara's early debut rumored. Bortoleto at home is the crowd story.

Goodyear brings a familiar setup to Homestead championship weekend: 11 prior uses in 2026, 12 Cup sets per team, and a blimp in Grand Theft Auto VI livery

NASCAR.com

NASCAR.com, Nov. 3: Goodyear brings a familiar tire setup to Homestead-Miami for Sunday’s Cup Championship Race (3 p.m. ET, NBC) — the left- and right-side tires have been used 11 times in 2026, including four times in the Chase, though this exact combination hasn’t run at Homestead. The O’Reilly Series uses the compound introduced at Darlington in September, another high-wear intermediate. Cup allotments: 12 sets (8 new for the race, 1 qualifying set that transfers, 3 for practice). The track’s variable banking plus the rubber the Eagles lay down should open multiple lanes. And the Goodyear Blimp flies a special Grand Theft Auto VI livery over the weekend — the game is set in Vice City, an hour from the track. In the O’Reilly title fight after Kvapil’s Martinsville win: Allgaier leads, then Love (-22), Day (-23), Kvapil (-31), Creed (-42), 200 laps Saturday (5 p.m. ET, The CW).

Why it matters: Homestead prep in rubber: a proven 11-use setup, 12 sets, multi-lane banking -- and the O'Reilly math (Allgaier clear, four chasers within 42) for Saturday's undercard title.

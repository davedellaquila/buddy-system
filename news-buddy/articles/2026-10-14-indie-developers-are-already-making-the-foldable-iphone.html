Indie developers are already making the foldable iPhone Duo sing

WebProNews, via TechCrunch and Axios · Oct 11

What this story is about

Ahead of the $1,999 iPhone Duo’s October 23 launch, a Bitrig hackathon in San Francisco produced 73 hinge-native prototypes, led by Duo-Man — indie dev Vidit Bhargava’s app that turns the Duo into a virtual Sony Walkman, with real cassette clicks recorded from actual hardware. Apple opened App Store submissions for Duo-optimized apps on October 5 via Xcode 27.1 (with an ’optimized for iPhone Duo’ badge), and new APIs like onHingeChange and ArrangementView let interfaces react to the fold angle in real time. From April 2027, every App Store submission must include iPhone Duo screenshots.

Why it matters

As both an Apple employee and a designer, the Duo is a brand-new hardware grammar — hinge-as-input, half-open postures — and indie devs probing it is the best preview of how the platform's design language will evolve.

Sharing Buddy

Sharing buddies with outside users — without throwing open every door.

● BRIEF REVIEW

I figure out how Buddies get shared with the people Dave cares about. Per-buddy shareable links, clear access levels, and real privacy — so he can hand Nancy the Party Buddy, or a collaborator one project, without exposing everything else. The static dashboard can't enforce access, so the real sharing lives in a backend — likely tied to the Buddy Suite story.

A note on privacy

Sharing is not publishing. Every share starts private, every link is scoped to one buddy, and Dave can revoke it in one tap. Family gets the warmth without the exposure — that's the whole point of me.

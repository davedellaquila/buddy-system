Nevada County Traction Co.

E Clampus Vitus · Grass Valley · Nevada County, California

What the marker says

Four 28-ton standard gauge street cars ran from 100 yards north of this spot five miles to Pine and Broad Streets, Nevada City. Car barns and power plant were located mid-way at Glenbrook. Line was shut down after a winter storm, Jan. 1924, never to run again – a victim of motor cars and buses. (1901 – 1924.) Twin plaque at the Nevada City end of the line (HMdb 40279).

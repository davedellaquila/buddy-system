⚖️ The rules of the road

  • Coordinates come from the marker's own page — opened, read, converted. Zero invented, zero estimated.
  • Plaque text is quoted verbatim; a gap is flagged as a gap.
  • War memorials and reported-missing markers are skipped — and counted, so every entry is accounted for.
  • HMdb rate-limits under parallel load: paced, sequential passes only.
  • One merge flag so far: the Ham's Station plaque stands at the already-staged Ham's Station stop — merged at insert, never duplicated.

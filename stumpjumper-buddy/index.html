Crank + bottom bracket removal — in progress Today

Strongarm II triple off the square-taper BB. Bolt (8 mm) out ~12:06 PM — the crank is not self-extracting, so Dave improvised with a puller from another set, pushing against the bolt left partway in. It worked: cranks off and BB out, reported ~12:45 PM. Finding: the BB is a Shimano BB-UN52-E (68 mm, BSA) — the -E means the FD plate clamps under the drive-side cup. Bike on the red Feedback Sports stand. Spindle measured ~12:50 PM: 114 mm end-to-end = nominal 113 — the BB spec is fully confirmed. Finding ~12:53 PM: the left adjusting cup's internal splines are chewed/rounded. Decision (Oct 24): replace, don't reuse — a Shimano BB-UN300 68×113 is ORDERED (Modern Bike, order #51680714, $26.99; ships Mon Oct 26, delivery a few business days after), and the Park BBT-22 tool order (Adrenaline Bike Works, $37.37) is completing behind it. When they arrive: BB swap + E-type FD plate check. Still open: the teardown's reason, and the reinstall itself (anti-seize on shell threads, taper dry).
